# Design → Review → repair

Use for a final interactive/high-fidelity candidate, an explicitly requested design-and-review workflow, or a repair based on an existing assessment.

Discover the companion through the host's available skill catalog by capability/name (`7viow-ux-review`, or the user's declared equivalent), then read its actual entry and relevant references. Do not rely on a machine-specific path. Respect a project-designated primary review method; use a single problem list and reuse applicable findings instead of issuing duplicate audits.

Pass the actual candidate/version and runnable entrance, user task/audience, source facts and unknowns, selected direction and rationale, locked decisions and permitted changes, Design System source/gaps, required interactions/states and known limitations. The reviewer applies its own assessment method and returns evidence, consequence/severity, minimal repair and observable acceptance. Do not give a purported independent reviewer the desired diagnosis as its answer.

Perform this review before claiming a final interactive/high-fidelity candidate has completed the design-review cycle. A host may apply both methods in one execution or use permitted independent execution; record which happened. Sequential self-review is not independent evaluation. Never claim a tool/skill was invoked when it was unavailable.

When the companion is unavailable, complete Design's own acceptance, report that independent Review has not run, and provide a portable handoff. This does not prevent an authorized design artifact from being delivered. No installation or new runtime is implied.

With modification authorized, fix substantive findings and verify their tasks plus affected regressions. Preserve finding IDs, selected direction and unaffected facts/behavior. A missing business decision is asked for, not invented. Deliver the actual revised artifact and the open/closed/untested findings; do not substitute an internal review table for the user-visible version.

## Optional writing collaboration

Interface labels, states, errors and short product guidance remain Design work. When the request also needs broader voice/concept exploration, prose editing, or a presentation narrative, use an available companion by capability: `7viow-writing-partner`, `7viow-writing-editor`, or `7viow-deck-writing-editor`, respectively. Follow the user's chosen method. A missing companion does not block Design's own deliverable; state any additional writing work outside the requested scope.

Pass current content/artifact, audience and goal, original sources, selected wording/direction, allowed changes and required output. Choose one owner to integrate the result. Before importing proposed copy into the interface, check necessary meaning, UI length constraints and actual action/state behavior. Preserve settled decisions during partial revisions; use existing material instead of reopening the whole workflow.
